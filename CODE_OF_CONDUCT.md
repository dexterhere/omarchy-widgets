# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a friendly, safe and welcoming place for everyone, whatever their experience level, background, identity, age, ability, nationality or beliefs. We pledge to act in ways that keep the community open, helpful and respectful.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- Accept feedback gracefully. Reviews exist to make the project better, including yours.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.
- Keep discussions on topic and in the right place (issues for bugs, discussions for ideas).
- Respect people's time. Maintainers are volunteers.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes, slurs or language aimed at a person or group.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information (addresses, accounts, credentials) without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or pressuring maintainers.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Security reports are different

Never post vulnerabilities, exploit details or other people's secrets publicly. Use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reporting a security problem in good faith is always welcome and is never a conduct violation.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, chats and events) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainers. Until a dedicated conduct address exists, use GitHub: open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/dexterhere/omarchy-widgets/security/advisories/new) titled "Conduct report" or contact [@dexterhere](https://github.com/dexterhere) directly. Reports are treated confidentially, and the reporter's identity is not shared without consent.

Maintainers may remove comments, close or lock threads, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban people whose behaviour breaks this code. We will usually start with a private conversation, and escalate for serious or repeated problems. If a maintainer is the subject of a report, another maintainer handles it.
